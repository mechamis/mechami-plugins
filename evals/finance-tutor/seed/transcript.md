## Q: What is the difference between a Treasury bill and a Treasury bond?

_2026-08-03 09:14 AM EDT_

Treasury bills (T-bills) are short-term securities that mature in one year or less and are sold at a discount to face value; the difference between the purchase price and the $1,000 face value is the interest earned. Treasury bonds are long-term securities with maturities of 20 or 30 years that pay interest every six months at a fixed coupon rate.

## Q: How is interest on Treasury securities taxed?

_2026-08-03 09:21 AM EDT_

Interest income from Treasury securities is subject to federal income tax but is exempt from state and local income taxes. The issuer reports it on Form 1099-INT.

**References**
